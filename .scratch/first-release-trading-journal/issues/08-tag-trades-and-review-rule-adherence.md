# 08: Tag trades and review rule adherence

**What to build:** Attach contextual tags and review whether a trade followed its rules, deviated, or remains unassessed, with notes and traceable corrections.

**Blocked by:** 03: Record and inspect a trade with its first entry.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec coverage:** User stories 16, 22, 23, 24, 25, 60, 61.

- [ ] Public operations attach and retrieve contextual tags without adding a separate strategy hierarchy.
- [ ] Support followed rules, deviated, and not assessed, with optional explanatory notes.
- [ ] Unassessed activity remains valid journal data.
- [ ] Retain previous values when correcting recorded review or tagging information.
- [ ] Both backends preserve tags, assessments, notes, and correction history across reopening, verified through the public API.
